THE GAME

Classic 3D action,
made for today.

SUPER COLLY (スーパーコリー) is an indie 3D action platformer inspired by the free exploration and playful movement of classic N64-era 3D games.

Explore stages, discover Noas, challenge athletic sections, search for hidden objectives, and build your own route through each world.

SUPER COLLY is being developed as a small-scale indie project with a strong focus on the feel of 3D action: camera behavior, movement, stage readability, performance, and approachable exploration.

This site will grow with the game. Development articles can be added here later to help players discover the project through topics such as stage design, Unity development, camera systems and game feel.
